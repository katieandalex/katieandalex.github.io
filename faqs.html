Travel

For flying we recommend Boston Logan Airport (BOS). It is less than a half hour drive from Lyman Estate and has car rentals as well as Uber/Lyft.

Lodging

While we do not have blocks at any particular hotel in the area, the following are great options. Most hotels are around 2.5 miles from the venue. Uber/Lyft are common in the area and there is plenty of parking both at the venue and near these amenities.

Waltham as a whole is a very nice and safe area, there are no particular areas (including nearby towns) that are unpleasant to spend time in.

  • Hampton Inn and Suites Boston/Waltham — 135 2nd Ave, Waltham, MA 02451
  • Hyatt House Boston/Waltham — 54 4th Ave, Waltham, MA 02451
  • Best Western Plus Waltham Boston — 380 Winter St, Waltham, MA 02451

Things to Do

New England in the Fall is the best!

In Waltham we can recommend taking a stroll around the Charles, cruising Moody St for restaurants and shops, or stopping by Waltham Community Farms for farm fresh produce.

Our favorite Waltham bites (caution: two years out of date by time of wedding): Peppino's Dosa, Gustazo's Cuban Kitchen, Lizzy's Homemade Icecream

If you're getting to the area early and want to explore fall activities, we can recommend Screeemfest at Canobie Lake Park, Salem (take the train!), and leaf peeping.

Costumes

We would really appreciate everyone coming in costume! If you don't want to do something complicated, here are some suggestions for accessories that can turn normal wedding attire into a costume:

  • Royalty: wear a crown, a cape, a scepter, a diadem
  • Masquerade: fun/fancy mask
  • Fairy/creature: wings, ears, capes, mushrooms
  • Animal: ears, nose, tail

Please also feel free to get creative with it! If you want to go as a pinball machine or caterpillar or deep sea diver we would love that.

The order of tables for dinner may or may not be affected by your table's ACS (Average Costume Score), so if you like food and winning consider creativity and commitment.

Please also feel free to wear costumes with white: as long as you are not dressed as a literal bride this is not an issue :)

The only things we would ask you to refrain from wearing are culturally insensitive costumes, guns (real or fake), and wedding dresses.
